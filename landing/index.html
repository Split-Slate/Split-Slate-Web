05 / Before you ask

The small
print, enlarged.

No mystery. No fine-print maze.

Can I start without an account?

Yes. Set up a local profile and start keeping expenses on your device—no email, password, or online account required for local use. Add the people involved so the app knows who paid and who shares each expense.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Adding expenses, all five split methods, balances, offline use, and exports are free core features. You can track local expenses without a subscription.

Does everyone get live updates?

Yes. In a synced group, expenses and balances update across connected devices. Keep track together as your group adds expenses, so everyone can follow the shared costs and see where they stand.

Does it really work offline?

Your first visit needs a connection to download the app’s offline files. Once those are ready, you can open the app and record expenses offline. Browser storage and caches aren’t guaranteed to last forever, so keep backups.

How do I install the app?

Open the app from this page. Use Install app in Settings or your browser’s installation menu. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu and Add to Home Screen. Options depend on the browser; a browser tab works too.

Can I send money through it?

No. Split Slate calculates balances and suggests repayments. Pay however you normally would, then record that payment in the group. The app doesn’t transfer or hold money.

What if I switch devices?

Open a synced group on another device to pick up its shared expenses and balances. For groups you keep locally, download a whole-app ZIP backup from Settings and restore it in the app on your new device. Keep a backup before clearing browser data.

What if everyone owes a different amount?

Use Unequal to set exact amounts, Shares for weighted contributions, or Percentage for a percentage-based split. Adjust lets you add or subtract from an equal share. You choose the participants for each expense—it doesn’t have to include everyone in the group.

Can more than one person pay for a bill?

Yes. Record each payer’s contribution to the same expense, then choose how the total is shared. What someone paid and what they owe are tracked separately, so contributing upfront doesn’t change their agreed share.

How does Split Slate work out who owes whom?

It compares what each person paid with their share of the expenses, then accounts for recorded repayments. In the weekend example, you paid ₹5,400 of a ₹9,000 trip. Your equal share is ₹3,000, so you’re owed ₹2,400: ₹600 from Maya and ₹1,800 from Dev.

Can I use a currency other than rupees?

Yes. Choose your group’s currency when you create it. Each group uses one currency so its expenses and balances stay consistent. Changing the currency label does not convert saved amounts or apply exchange rates.

What’s the difference between exporting and backing up?

A group export gives you a snapshot as a link, CSV, or ZIP—useful for sharing a copy or working with the data outside the app. A whole-app ZIP backup is for restoring your saved local data. Keep both a useful export and a recent backup when the records matter to you.
